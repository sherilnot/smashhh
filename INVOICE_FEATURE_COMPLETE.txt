┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKFLOW OVERVIEW                                                       │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Morning:
  📝 Manager creates checklist → Sends to warehouse
  
Afternoon:  
  🚚 Warehouse delivers items (may not match exact quantities)
  📋 Manager creates invoice with actual received quantities
  📧 Manager submits invoice to RM001
  
Evening:
  👔 RM001 reviews all invoices from all stores
  🔍 RM001 sees discrepancies highlighted
  📊 RM001 takes action (external to system)
